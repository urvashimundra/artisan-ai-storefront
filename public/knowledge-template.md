# My shop notes

Fill in only what applies to your shop. Delete the examples and write your own. Short, plain sentences work best.
You can write in English, Gujarati, Hindi, or a mix. Any language works.

Customers can learn what you write here through the assistant's answers. Do not write bank details, passwords or private information.

## About us
Who you are, where you work, how long you have been making things.
Example: We are a family of potters in Morbi. We have made clay lamps and pots for 20 years.

## Ordering process
How a customer orders and what happens next.
Example: Choose a product, press Buy now and pay by UPI. We check the payment and confirm your order on WhatsApp.

## Delivery and shipping
Where you deliver, how long it takes, what it costs.
Example: We ship across India in 5 to 7 days. Delivery is free above Rs 1000.

## Returns and refunds
What you accept back and when.
Example: Handmade items are not returned. If a piece arrives broken, send a photo within 2 days and we will replace it.

## Custom orders (colours, sizes, names, lead time)
What can be changed, and how long it takes.
Example: We can paint your name on a pot. Colours: red, blue, white. It takes 10 days.

## Bulk orders and discounts
Write each discount as a clear rule: a number of items and a percentage.
Example:
- 10 or more: 5% off
- 25 or more: 10% off

## Gifting and packaging
Gift wrap, notes, boxes, wedding or festival orders.
Example: Free gift wrap. We can add a handwritten note.

## Care instructions
How to clean and look after the product.
Example: Wash by hand with mild soap. Do not use in a microwave.

## Materials and sizes
What the products are made of and the usual sizes.
Example: Natural clay, no plastic. Small diya 5 cm, large diya 9 cm.

## Making time and dispatch
How long it takes to make, and when you send.
Example: In-stock items are sent in 2 days. Made-to-order items take 7 to 10 days.

## Payment
Which payment you accept.
Example: UPI only. We confirm the payment by hand, so please send the UTR number.

## Contact hours and languages
When and how customers can reach you.
Example: Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm. We speak Gujarati, Hindi and English.

## FAQs
Questions customers often ask, with your answers.
Example: Q: Is the paint safe for food? A: No, painted pots are for decoration only.

## Anything else
Anything else a customer should know.
Example: We are closed for Diwali from 10 to 20 November.
